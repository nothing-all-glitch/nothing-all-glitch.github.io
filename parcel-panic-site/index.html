A LITTLE FACTORY. A LOT TO SORT.

Find your rhythm.
Then route the rush.

Tap either rotary switch to change the route. Orange circles go left, cyan triangles go down the middle, and purple squares go right. The glowing conveyor shows the branch you've selected.

Work through 60 shifts as seven parcel types bring new twists. Chase three stars, collect stamps and change the look of your depot. Come back for the daily challenge, or see how long you can keep an endless run moving.

YOUR SHIFT. YOUR SETTINGS.

Take the game at your pace.

Play the core game offline. Tune music, sound and haptics, or turn on reduced motion. Your progress stays on your device.

The Android game includes optional rewarded bonuses and occasional ads between runs. Ads need an internet connection. You can turn off future game ad requests and review available privacy choices in Settings. No account or in-app purchase is required.
