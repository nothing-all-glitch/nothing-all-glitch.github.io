Game data on your device

One Pixel stores your best precision, levels cleared, daily results, selected and unlocked skins, unlock notices, sound, haptics and motion settings, your coin balance, daily gift streak, daily mission progress, skins bought with coins, and whether Remove Ads is active on your device. It also stores completed-run counts, ad timing, rewarded-unlock dates and recent reward transaction identifiers to enforce ad limits and avoid duplicate rewards. These are game records, not payment or account records.

Android uses private app Preferences storage, with migration of older WebView saves. The browser game uses local browser storage. This state is not sent to a developer backend. One Pixel has no account system, cloud score service or online leaderboard; no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included in the reviewed version.

In-app purchases

One Pixel offers optional one-time purchases (Remove Ads, a Starter Pack and coin packs). Google Play processes every payment; the developer never receives your card, bank or billing details. To deliver and restore purchases, the app asks Google Play Billing which of this app’s products your Google account owns, and stores the product name and a shortened purchase receipt on your device so the same purchase is never granted twice. The developer has no server and does not receive purchase receipts. Google Play keeps your payment information and purchase history under Google’s privacy policy.

Coins are stored only on your device. Coins you spent, or bought in coin packs, are not restored after you uninstall the app or clear its storage. One-time purchases (Remove Ads and the Starter Pack, including its coins and Gold skin) can be restored from the store screen.

Ratings prompt

After a good moment in the game (for example a cleared Challenge or a finished Daily), the app may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating card, at most once every 30 days. Google Play decides whether the card appears. The app does not learn whether you rated it or what you wrote.

Advertising and third-party data

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). AdMob provides interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. When no rewarded ad can be shown, the game offers the same reward without an ad. Google can process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers such as Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in device accounts.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP processes consent information. SDK processing means this app cannot be described as collecting no data, even though game progress stays on your device. Development test ads may also contact Google.

See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure. Processing depends on the SDK version, consent and advertising configuration.

Ads and consent choices

The game requests updated consent information and checks UMP’s permission to request ads before initializing ads and at ad-request boundaries. Where a consent form is required and available through Google’s configured messaging service, the game presents it. If UMP indicates that privacy options are required, a Privacy options entry is available in the game’s Settings to reopen the form. The choices offered depend on your region and Google’s messaging configuration. Gameplay remains available if ads or consent forms are unavailable.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID through Android’s privacy controls where supported. Declining personalized advertising or changing an identifier does not necessarily stop all limited-ad, diagnostic or fraud-prevention processing.

Replays, sharing and permissions

Replays are generated locally from the game’s rendered canvas and game-generated audio. One Pixel does not use your camera or microphone. When you choose to share a replay, Android grants your chosen destination app access to that clip. Sharing a daily result sends the result text you choose. The developer does not upload these clips or results; destination apps handle your shared information under their own policies.

Network access supports ads, consent and Google Play Billing. The Google Play Billing library declares Android’s billing permission so the app can offer purchases. Optional haptics use vibration. The ads SDK adds advertising-related permissions. The app does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or broad file-storage access. Replay sharing uses Android’s controlled file-sharing mechanism.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear Android app storage or uninstall the app. Browser game progress can be removed by clearing that site’s data. Android backup is disabled and the game does not offer save synchronization. Deletion resets your progress, settings and unlocks.

Temporary replay files use the app’s private cache. On startup and export, the app attempts to remove files older than 24 hours and files beyond the five most recent exports. Cleanup occurs when those operations run, rather than at a guaranteed deletion time; Android may also clear cache. Copies you save or share elsewhere are unaffected.

Google’s retention and deletion practices follow its policies and available controls. Clearing game data does not delete data already processed by Google or another app. There is no developer-operated account database to delete.

Security

Game saves and replay cache use Android’s private app storage. Shared clips use controlled file access. The Android app disables cleartext network traffic, and Google states its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts collected data in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Children and intended audience

One Pixel is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over, and is not intended for children under 13. The game does not collect a birth date or offer account registration. An intended audience of 13+ does not establish that every player is above the age of consent for personalized advertising in their region. Google’s consent and privacy choices depend on regional requirements and available messaging controls. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information processed while a child used the app, contact the developer at the email below; Google’s own privacy controls apply to data it processes.

This privacy website

This static website contains no analytics scripts, cookies, embedded ads, external fonts or trackers. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses for security, and may process request and device information under its privacy statement. The developer has added no visitor analytics or tracking. Hosting-provider retention and deletion follow that provider’s policies and controls.
