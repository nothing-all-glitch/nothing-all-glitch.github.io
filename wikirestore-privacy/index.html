Game data on your device

Wiki Restore saves your progress on your device: the pages you have restored, your Sorting Points and Completion Points, installed scripts, Talk page messages you have read, wiki notes you have found, achievements, challenge records, unlocked skins, any running coffee break, and your sound, theme, text-size and vibration settings. These are game records, not account or payment records.

The save is kept in the app’s private storage. Wiki Restore has no account system, developer server, cloud save or online leaderboard, and includes no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The developer does not receive your save.

Advertising and third-party data

Wiki Restore includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). AdMob provides optional rewarded ads in two places only: a coffee break that doubles Sort Daemon’s speed for ten minutes, and Collect x2 on the welcome-back card. An ad plays only when you tap to watch one. There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads.

When ads are allowed, Google can process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP processes your consent choices. Because of this SDK processing, the app cannot be described as collecting no data, even though your save stays on your device.

See Google’s privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure. Processing depends on the SDK version, your consent and the advertising configuration.

Ads and consent choices

Each time the app opens, it asks Google’s UMP for up-to-date consent information and requests no ads until UMP allows it. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), a consent form is shown before any ad. Where UMP says it is required, Ad privacy choices under Settings › Privacy and ads reopens the form so you can change your choice. US users can also opt out of the sale or sharing of their data through Google’s privacy message where it applies. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Ads are optional: the whole game, including its story and credits, is playable without watching any. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s privacy settings where supported. Declining personalized advertising or changing an identifier does not necessarily stop all limited-ad, diagnostic or fraud-prevention processing.

Permissions

Network access is used only for ads and consent; the game itself works offline. The ads SDK adds advertising-related permissions, such as access to the advertising ID and Android’s privacy-preserving ad services. Light haptics can be turned off in Settings. The app sends no notifications and does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, or access to your photos or files.

Retention and deletion

Your save remains until you use Reset progress in the game’s Settings, clear Wiki Restore’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the save in your device backup and restore it on a new phone; this backup is handled by Google under your account’s backup settings, and the developer cannot access it.

Google’s retention and deletion of data processed by the ads SDK follows Google’s policies and the controls it provides. Clearing game data does not delete data already processed by Google. There is no developer-operated account or database to delete data from.

Security

The save uses Android’s private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Google states its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it collects in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Children and intended audience

Wiki Restore is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask for a birth date and offers no account registration, chat or way to share personal information with other players; the Talk page holds scripted messages from fictional characters. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact the developer at the email below. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes everything the app itself stores, and Google’s privacy controls apply to data it processes.

This privacy website

This static website contains no analytics scripts, cookies, embedded ads, external fonts or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses for security, and may process request and device information under its privacy statement.
