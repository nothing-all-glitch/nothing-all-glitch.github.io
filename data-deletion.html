Deoldify Data Deletion and Privacy Help

Deoldify does not currently provide user accounts and does not operate a cloud photo storage service.

Delete local app data

You can remove Deoldify's local app data by using your Android device settings:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select Deoldify.
  4. Open Storage.
  5. Choose Clear cache or Clear data.

You can also uninstall Deoldify to remove app-local files, including the downloaded model file.

Delete saved images

If you saved generated images to your gallery, delete them from your device gallery or file manager. Saved images are controlled by your device after you save them.

Ads and Google data

Deoldify uses Google AdMob for advertising. To manage ad personalization and advertising identifiers, use your Android or Google account privacy/ad settings. Google handles advertising-related data according to Google's policies.
