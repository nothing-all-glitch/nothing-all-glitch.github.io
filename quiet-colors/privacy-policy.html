Privacy Policy for Quiet Colors

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Quiet Colors is provided by VectorShift Digital. Contact: masoodbarkiwork@gmail.com.

Quiet Colors is a coloring app for teens and adults aged 13 and older. Editable drawings stay on your device. There is no Quiet Colors account system or cloud drawing backup. Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. A one-time complete-library purchase uses Google Play Billing when available.

Information stored on your device

The app stores drawings, color choices, progress, settings, local rewarded-design unlocks and purchase ownership records to provide coloring and restore access. It stores an optional age-group choice for ad eligibility, not your birth date. Quiet Colors does not upload your drawings to a developer-operated server or send them to advertisers. If you export a PNG, it goes to the destination you select through Android; that destination's privacy practices apply.

Optional advertising and consent

Rewarded ads are offered only after you choose the adult age group, 18 or older. Preparing or watching an ad is optional. The app does not request ads for an unknown age group or a choice under 18. Coloring free designs and existing saved work does not require an ad.

Google's ad and consent services can process IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app and ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or account-related identifiers. These support advertising, measurement, consent management and fraud prevention. The app removes the Android advertising-ID permission, but this does not prevent all identifier processing. Data use depends on consent choices, region and Google services. Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Before an optional ad request, the app updates consent information and presents Google's required consent form when applicable. Privacy options are available in Settings when Google requires that entry point. Choosing the adult age group is not itself advertising consent. Learn more at How Google uses information from apps and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and Restore

Google Play handles checkout, payment details and purchase records under its policies. Quiet Colors receives purchase information including product identifiers, purchase tokens, receipt data, signatures and purchase state. It checks signed receipts locally, stores ownership on your device and communicates with Google Play to acknowledge completed purchases and restore owned purchases. The app does not receive payment-card details or send receipts to a separate developer-operated verification service.

Restore uses the relevant Google Play account. It does not restore drawings or rewarded unlocks from another installation. Clearing local app data does not delete Google Play's transaction records. Private review builds keep production checkout disabled until setup and testing are complete.

Retention and deletion

Drawings and settings remain in local app storage until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local drawings and rewarded unlocks. Exported PNGs remain in their chosen destination until you delete them there. Local purchase records can be recreated through Play Restore when an eligible purchase is still owned. Google retains service, consent, advertising and purchase information according to its policies and settings.

Security and permissions

Quiet Colors uses Android app-private storage and platform protections. It uses network access for Google services. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children and your choices

Quiet Colors is not directed to children under 13. Users aged 13–17 can use it without rewarded ads. You can avoid optional ads, change the age-group choice, review required privacy options, delete local drawings and exported files, or contact us with privacy questions. There is no Quiet Colors account to delete.
