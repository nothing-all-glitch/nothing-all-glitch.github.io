YOUR INFORMATION & YOUR CHOICES

Privacy policy

Parcel Panic · Developer: Masood Ur Rehman
Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Android version 1.2.0

Your game progress and settings stay on your device. The Android game uses Google advertising and consent services, which can process information when online. There is no game account, in-app purchase or developer-operated analytics service.

1. Local game information

Parcel Panic stores completed shifts, scores, stars, stamps, cosmetic unlocks, daily and endless progress, sound and accessibility settings, the optional age-treatment choice and reward credits on your device. These game saves are not sent to the developer. No name, date of birth or account login is requested to play.

The app does not operate a cloud-save service. Android app backup is disabled in this version. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it can permanently remove your local progress. Reset progress in the game clears game progress; your optional age-treatment preference is retained. Google consent choices are managed separately and are not reset by the local progress reset. If you use a browser edition, its save stays in that browser's local storage and can be removed through browser settings.

2. Advertising and consent services

The Android game uses Google's AdMob GMA Next-Gen SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) for rewarded bonuses, interstitial ads at finished-result exits, and consent or privacy choices. Campaign shifts 1–5 remain free of interstitials, including replays. After those five shifts have each been cleared, the app attempts an interstitial at every eligible finished-result exit from shift 6 onward, including wins, losses, replays and unlocked daily or endless runs. There is no app cooldown between ads, and ads are not shown during gameplay. Ads require an internet connection, current consent and a ready ad; if an ad is not ready, the exit continues without an ad or a delayed interruption. The core game remains playable offline. Rewarded bonuses remain optional: you can choose to complete a rewarded ad for 15 extra coins. This version has no banner ads, in-app purchases, game accounts or developer-operated backend, analytics or crash-upload service.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. These can include an advertising ID, app-set ID and, where applicable, account identifiers associated with signed-in devices. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit. Processing depends on consent, age treatment, device settings and SDK behavior.

See Google's GMA Next-Gen SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for further information about Google's services.

3. Consent and ad choices

Open the game's Settings to choose optional Ad privacy age treatment, read Privacy, or review Ad choices where Google's consent system makes those choices available. The game updates consent information when starting. Required consent forms are opened through an explicit privacy action or a request for an optional ad; they do not block starting the game.

The Optional ads switch has been removed in version 1.2.0; there is no app setting that turns off both ad types for free. This update preserves saved age-treatment preferences and Google's consent choices. Ad requests and presentation remain subject to current consent, age treatment and SDK safeguards. Privacy choices do not delete information Google already processed or necessarily stop every type of SDK diagnostic processing.

Android's advertising-ID controls may let you reset or delete that identifier. These controls do not remove every type of information an advertising service may process. Google's privacy policy explains additional controls and data requests. The developer cannot delete records held by Google through the game's local reset control.

4. Audience and optional age treatment

The intended audience is ages 13 and up. No mandatory first-run age prompt or date-of-birth collection is used. The optional age-treatment choice is saved on your device and is used to configure age-restriction and under-age-of-consent flags with Google. The literal age-band label is not sent to those APIs. You can keep Prefer not to say in every region and continue playing with conservative restriction flags; those flags do not verify or establish your actual age. It does not gate gameplay or verify your age; the game does not request your date of birth or exact birthday.

  • Prefer not to say (default) or Under 16: conservative restricted treatment, including under-age-of-consent and child-directed ad request settings.
  • 16–17: teen ad treatment, subject to consent requirements.
  • 18+: standard age treatment, subject to consent requirements.

Every age choice uses a general-audience ad content limit. Restricted or unknown-age treatment can reduce ad availability. An ages-13-plus audience does not mean every player is legally able to consent to personalized advertising.

5. Offline play, permissions and sharing

Game code, artwork and audio are bundled for offline play. Internet and network-state access support ads and consent. Advertising-related permissions and SDK components support identifiers, basic device or network information, wake locks and foreground services. Haptics use vibration support.

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, shared-storage, notification or billing permission. An IP address can still indicate approximate location without a location permission.

When you choose to share a result, the game opens your device's share sheet with result text. You choose the receiving app and recipient; that app's own privacy practices apply. The developer does not automatically receive that result.

6. This website and support email

This privacy page and the developer website are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies added by the developer. GitHub may process request and device information to provide and secure hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email masoodbarkiwork@gmail.com, Masood Ur Rehman receives your email address and the information you choose to send, and uses it to respond to your question or request. The email provider also processes that message under its own policies. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.
