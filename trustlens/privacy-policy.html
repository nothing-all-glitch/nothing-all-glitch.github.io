Privacy Policy for TrustLens

Last updated: October 17, 2026

TrustLens is provided by VectorShift Digital. Contact: masoodbarkiwork@gmail.com.

TrustLens checks one suspicious message, link, screenshot, QR code or PDF at a time and gives a plain verdict. There is no TrustLens account. Nothing you share is sent until you tap Check now, and the app shows exactly what a check will send before you do.

What stays on your phone

Photos, screenshots, QR codes and PDFs are read on your phone. Only the text you see in the box, and the links you leave ticked, are sent for a check. Whole PDFs are never uploaded. A photo is sent only if you turn on Include an image for that single check; it is re-encoded and its metadata (such as location) is removed first. Copies of shared files are kept in the app's private storage while you work and deleted within 15 minutes, or when the check finishes or is cancelled.

History is off by default. If you turn on Save history, the app keeps verdict summaries (risk level, score, when and what kind of item) for 30 days, encrypted with a key held by your phone. It never keeps the message, links or images. History is excluded from Android backups.

What the TrustLens server keeps

The server never stores the text, links or images you check, and keeps them out of its logs. A finished result is kept for 10 minutes so the app can reconnect if your network drops; it is encrypted with a key that only your phone has. App integrity and purchases

To prevent abuse, the app asks Google Play Integrity to confirm it was installed from Google Play and is running on a genuine device; Google processes device and app information to answer. TrustLens+ is sold through Google Play Billing: Google handles checkout and payment details, and TrustLens receives the purchase state and a purchase token to confirm your subscription. The token is stored encrypted and never logged. TrustLens never receives your payment card details.

Permissions

TrustLens uses internet access to run checks. It reads only the items you share with it or pick with Android's photo and file pickers, and does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions. It shows no ads and has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your choices and deletion

You decide what each check sends: untick links, edit the text, and leave the optional switches off. You can turn history off or delete it in the app at any time. Uninstalling or clearing the app's storage removes everything on your phone; the installation record on the server is deleted after 90 days without use. Because TrustLens has no accounts, we can't find that record from your email address; if you still have the app and want it removed sooner, email us and we'll help. Step-by-step instructions are on the support page. For any privacy question, email us.

Children

TrustLens is not directed to children under 13.

Where information is processed

The TrustLens server and its database run in the United States. Google and Anthropic may process information in other countries under their own terms.
