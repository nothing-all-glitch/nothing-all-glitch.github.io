Colorize

Turn black-and-white pictures into natural color with one tap, using an on-device colorization model.

Fix fading

Restore the contrast and true colors of prints that turned red, magenta or yellow with age. Sepia comes back to clean gray.

Erase objects

Paint over a person, a date stamp or a stain. The gap is filled from the surrounding photo, on the phone.

Repair

Scratches, dust and tears are found automatically or painted by hand, then filled in from the photo.

Upscale

2× or 4× up to 12 MP with Real-ESRGAN, so small scans print sharp. A Detail slider sharpens any photo.

Scan a print

Photograph a print straight from the album; edges and perspective are corrected automatically.

Private by design

Colorization, fading fix, erasing, repair and upscaling all run on the device. The internet is used only to download the colorization model once, for ads and consent, and for the optional one-time DeOldify Plus purchase that removes ads and makes Repair, Erase and Upscale unlimited.
